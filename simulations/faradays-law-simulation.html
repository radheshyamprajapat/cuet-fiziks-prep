Faraday's Law of Electromagnetic Induction

Whenever the magnetic flux linked with a coil changes, an emf is induced in the coil.

ε = −N dΦB/dt
  1. Move the magnet toward the coil.
  2. Magnetic flux through the coil changes.
  3. An emf is induced in the coil.
  4. Increasing the magnet speed increases the rate of change of flux.
  5. Increasing the number of turns increases the induced emf.
  6. The negative sign represents Lenz's Law.
