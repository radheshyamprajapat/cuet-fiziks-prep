Einstein's Photoelectric Equation

When light of sufficient frequency falls on a metal surface, electrons are emitted.

Kmax = hν − φ
  1. Each photon has energy E = hν.
  2. A minimum frequency is required to overcome the work function of the metal.
  3. If ν < ν₀, no photoelectrons are emitted.
  4. If ν > ν₀, photoelectrons are emitted.
  5. Increasing frequency increases the maximum kinetic energy of electrons.
  6. Increasing intensity increases the number of emitted electrons, not their maximum kinetic energy.
