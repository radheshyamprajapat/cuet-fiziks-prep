About CUET Fiziks Prep

CUET Fiziks Prep is an educational platform dedicated to helping students prepare for the Common University Entrance Test (CUET) Physics. Our mission is to make Physics simple, conceptual, and exam-oriented through quality study material and expert guidance.

Our Mission

To provide affordable and high-quality Physics education for every CUET aspirant through notes, previous year questions, practice tests, and strategy sessions.

What We Offer

📘 Notes

Chapter-wise concise notes for quick revision.

📝 PYQs

Previous Year Questions with detailed solutions.

🎯 Strategy

Preparation plans, exam tips and revision guidance.

🎥 Video Lectures

Concept-based learning through YouTube.
